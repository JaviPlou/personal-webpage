About

I build sensors that listen to cells — combining surface‑enhanced Raman spectroscopy, plasmonic nanomaterials and AI to read the chemical language of cancer in real time.

Trained as a biotechnologist (UNIZAR) and PhD in Biomedicine (UPV‑EHU, with the Liz‑Marzán lab at CIC biomaGUNE), I've spent eight years at the intersection of materials, biology and data. Today I lead a postdoctoral line at CIC nanoGUNE on AI‑assisted optical biosensing for ATMP / CAR‑T manufacturing — work I'm now scaling commercially as co‑founder & CTO of Prospect Biotech.
